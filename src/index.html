WHAT CAREGRID BRINGS TOGETHER

More than a dashboard.

CareGrid is designed as an RPM-focused patient and care-management workspace, connecting the data coming from home with the people responsible for reviewing and acting on it.

  • Patient profiles & conditionsKeep monitoring context, programmes and relevant care information together.
  • Vitals & longitudinal trendsReview readings over time rather than one measurement at a time.
  • Programme configurationConfirm available alert rules and response arrangements during setup. Surface readings that may require review according to configured care rules.
  • Care plans & follow-upRecord planned actions, tasks and care-team activity.
  • RPM patient CRMTrack relationships, outreach, programme participation and patient engagement around remote care.
  • Audit & role controlsSupport accountable workflows with user roles and activity history.
IN SIMPLE TERMS

Your device measures. CareGrid organises. Your care team gets a clearer view.

CareGrid does not replace a healthcare professional or emergency care. It is designed to make remote monitoring information easier to review, prioritise and use within an appropriate care programme.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Clear answers for your next step.

Can I get help choosing equipment?

Yes. Start a device enquiry with the model or monitoring need you’re considering. We’ll discuss availability, accessories and setup requirements. Your clinician guides clinical suitability and your care plan.

Will every device connect to CareGrid?

Compatibility depends on the exact device and the available connection. Ask us to confirm support for your device, app and intended workflow before committing.

What should our care team check in a demo?

Discuss supported integrations, staff roles, onboarding, data handling and who reviews incoming readings. Agree the scope and responsibilities for your programme.

How do equipment support requests work?

Start with the equipment make, model and fault. Assessment, logistics, repair or calibration availability and any charges are confirmed for the specific device.
