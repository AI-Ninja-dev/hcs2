CONTINUOUS GLUCOSE MONITORING · YUWELL ANYTIME CT3

A focused CGM system for continuous glucose visibility.

The Yuwell Anytime CT3 combines a 14-day sensor with a reusable rechargeable transmitter and Bluetooth-connected app, helping adults view glucose readings, trends and alerts throughout daily life.

  • Up to 14 days of sensor wear
  • Reusable rechargeable transmitter
  • Bluetooth data delivery to the supported app
  • Glucose trend and high/low alert visibility
Availability Confirm current CT3 stock Support Compatibility and setup guidance available
Yuwell · Anytime CT3
Sensor→Phone→Glucose trends→CareGrid→Care team

For adults 18+. Use the CT3 in accordance with its instructions for use and your healthcare professional’s guidance. CGM readings reflect interstitial glucose and may differ from capillary blood-glucose measurements, particularly during rapid glucose change.
